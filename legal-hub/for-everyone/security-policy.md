# Security Policy

**Last updated:** 30 September 2026

**Security testing of Keepnet systems is not permitted without prior written authorisation from Keepnet.** This policy is not an invitation to scan or test our websites, platform, APIs, add-ins, applications or infrastructure. Keepnet does not run a bug bounty programme.

### 1. Legal notice

This policy does not grant permission under the Computer Misuse Act 1990, or any equivalent law in another country, to access, attempt to access, scan or interact with Keepnet systems.

This policy does not permit any action that breaches the law, or that could cause Keepnet to breach its legal obligations, including the Computer Misuse Act 1990, the Investigatory Powers Act 2016, the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

### 2. Scope

This policy applies to keepnetlabs.com, all of its subdomains, and all other domains, applications and infrastructure owned or operated by Keepnet.

### 3. Security testing requires written authorisation

Security testing of any Keepnet system is permitted only with prior written authorisation signed by Keepnet. This includes:

* automated vulnerability scanning
* penetration testing
* fuzzing
* credential testing
* denial of service
* social engineering of Keepnet employees

To request authorisation, email [security@keepnetlabs.com](mailto:security@keepnetlabs.com) **before you start**, with:

* your name and organisation
* the systems you want to test
* your planned methods and tools
* your testing window
* the IP addresses you will test from

Do not start testing until you receive a signed authorisation letter from Keepnet.

**Keepnet customers** who want to test their own account should contact their Customer Success Manager. Customer testing is carried out under the Keepnet Security Testing Rules of Engagement.

### 4. Reporting an issue you noticed

If you notice a security issue during normal use of our services, report it to [security@keepnetlabs.com](mailto:security@keepnetlabs.com) with:

* the affected URL or component
* steps to reproduce
* the impact

When reporting, you must not:

* access, download, modify or keep data that does not belong to you
* run further tests to confirm or expand the issue
* share the issue with anyone until we confirm it is resolved

We acknowledge valid reports within 5 business days.

### 5. No bug bounty

Keepnet does not run a bug bounty programme and does not pay for reports. We do not respond to requests for payment.

A demand for payment in exchange for vulnerability details, or a threat to publish a vulnerability or data, will be referred to law enforcement.

### 6. Out of scope

We will not act on reports that only list output from automated tools, or findings without demonstrated impact, including:

* missing security headers
* TLS/SSL configuration
* SPF, DKIM or DMARC records
* clickjacking on pages without sensitive actions
* software version disclosure
* self-XSS
* rate limiting on non-sensitive endpoints

### 7. Good faith reports

If you report an issue you noticed during normal use of our services, in line with this policy and without active testing or payment demands, Keepnet will not take legal action against you in respect of that report.

This does not apply to testing carried out without written authorisation.

### 8. Your rights

This policy does not limit any rights or protections that apply to you under the law of your country. Keepnet reserves all of its legal rights.
